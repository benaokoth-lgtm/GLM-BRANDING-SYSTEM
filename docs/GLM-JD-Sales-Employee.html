Sales Employee

Wins, captures and follows up customer orders across DTF, UV, embroidery, large-format and laser work, and is paid on what customers pay.

1Purpose of the role

The Sales Employee brings in work and turns customer needs into correctly priced, correctly recorded orders. They advise customers on the right product and process, capture each order in the system, make sure it is paid for, follow it through production and quality control, and keep the customer informed until it is handed over. They protect the company's prices and margins, build a book of repeat clients, and are accountable for their own monthly sales.

2Key responsibilities

Selling and advising

  • Understand what the customer needs and advise on the right process and product: DTF transfers (by the metre or per piece), embroidery (priced by stitch count), UV printing, large-format banners, vinyl and posters, laser engraving, and branded garments and merchandise.
  • Explain prices, timelines and what the customer must supply (artwork, garments, sizes, quantities).
  • Bring in new clients and grow repeat business from existing ones.

Capturing orders accurately

  • Capture every order in the system the same day: General Order, DTF Film Order, DTF Artwork Order, UV DTF Film Order, UV DTF Artwork Order or Embroidery Order.
  • Price from the price list only: services and materials as separate lines, artwork size in square metres where the service is priced by area, the heat-press fee on every piece that needs one, stitch counts and quantities on embroidery, and the supplier's real quote on contracted-out work.
  • Record customer name and phone whenever the client is to be credited to you or is a repeat client.

Crediting clients honestly

  • Answer Who brought this order? truthfully. Claim a client only when you genuinely brought them through your own network; they are then credited to you for the ownership period (12 months at present).
  • Never claim a client who belongs to someone else, and never move an order to another person or to a freelance sales person to gain or avoid credit.

Taking payment

  • Take payment through the order screen — cash, M-Pesa (STK push), bank transfer or card, singly or split — and make sure a receipt is issued.
  • Record deposits and balances as they arrive; only money recorded in the system counts as a sale. Never accept or hold payment outside the system.
  • For orders paid on completion, collect before hand-over, or follow the approved credit route.

Following the order through

  • Check order status (Order Received → In Production → Quality Check → Ready → Completed) and keep the customer informed.
  • Work production jobs assigned to you when asked: Start and Finish them with the true units produced.
  • Hand completed orders to the customer once they have passed quality control. If a balance is owing, take the payment first or use the credit route approved by management.

Collections and follow-up

  • Chase balances on your orders and collect them before the month ends.
  • Send the customer their receipt or invoice by email or WhatsApp from the order screen where appropriate.
  • Give Finance what it needs to quote corporate clients (requirements, quantities, contacts).

Prices, discounts and approvals

  • Sell at list price. Keep discounts within the standard ceiling; anything above it needs supervisor approval before the order proceeds.
  • On DTF artwork and embroidery, a price below the recommended or stitch price is a discount that a manager must approve first; you cannot approve your own.
  • Never lower a price to reach a pay band. The minimum prices stay as they are.

3Authority and limits

What you can do in the system is set by your role and is checked on the server at every request.

You can

  • Capture General, Film, Artwork and Embroidery orders
  • See and manage the orders you captured
  • Take and record payments on your orders
  • Print, email or WhatsApp receipts
  • Hand over finished orders (on credit only as management allows)
  • Work production jobs assigned to you
  • Record DTF film sales and artwork jobs
  • See your own commission and the pay guide
  • Choose your colour scheme; change your own PIN

You need approval for

  • Discounts above the standard ceiling (supervisor)
  • Artwork priced below the recommended price (manager)
  • Embroidery priced below the stitch or quantity price (manager)
  • Refunds, credit notes and corrections to a recorded sale (Finance)

You cannot

  • See other people's orders or supplier costs and mark-ups
  • Change prices, bands, rates or settings
  • Approve your own price cuts or commission
  • Mark an order completed except by handing it over after quality control
  • Open Finance, Compliance or Accounting
  • Delete or edit recorded payments

5Remuneration

  • Your pay for a month is the payout of the pay band your net sales reached — a step, not a slice — and never less than the minimum wage. Film and artwork extras are paid on top, as the customer pays.
  • The month is approved by a manager and your pay goes through payroll; payday is the 5th of each month, for the month before. PAYE, NSSF, SHIF and the housing levy are deducted and shown on your payslip.
  • The bands, target, minimum wage and payday are set by an Admin. The figures in force are always shown on Commission → How you are paid, which is written from the live settings.
At the date of issue — October 2026 11 pay bands from the minimum wage up to KES 60,000 a month; band 4 (KES 120,000 to 179,999) pays KES 35,000; minimum wage KES 16,113.75; payday the 5th. If these have changed, the screen above is right and this note is out of date.

6Standards and conduct in the system

  • Your PIN is yours. Never share it, never sign in as someone else, and sign out when you leave the screen.
  • Every record is traceable. The system records who captured, changed or approved each order, payment and claim; accuracy and honesty in what you enter are part of the job.
  • One order, one record. No splitting or hiding of sales, no side payments, no unrecorded discounts.
  • Customer information is confidential and used only for the order and its follow-up.
  • Raise problems early — a wrong price, a disputed payment, a quality issue — with your supervisor or Finance.

8Typical requirements

  • Comfort with numbers: prices, quantities, areas, discounts and balances.
  • Good communication and customer care; able to explain technical options simply.
  • Honesty and attention to detail in recording orders and payments.
  • Able to use a computer or tablet and a till-style order screen; training is given.
  • Interest in printing, branding and garment decoration; experience in sales or customer service is an advantage.
  • Self-motivated and able to work towards a monthly sales target.
