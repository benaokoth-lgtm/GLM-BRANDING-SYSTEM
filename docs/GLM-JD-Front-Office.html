Front Office Officer

The first face of the shop: receives customers, captures orders for the sales persons, takes and records every payment, and hands finished work over.

1Purpose of the role

The Front Office Officer is the customer's first and last contact. You welcome and direct customers, capture orders for the sales persons and for freelance sales persons, take every payment and record it correctly, issue receipts, keep customers informed, and hand finished orders over once they have passed quality control. You are the control point for money coming in at the counter: what is paid must be recorded, exactly and the same day.

2Key responsibilities

Welcoming customers and enquiries

  • Greet and direct customers; answer enquiries on products, prices, timelines and what the customer must bring.
  • Refer technical or custom requests to the sales person or the Supervisor.

Capturing orders for sales persons

  • Capture General, Film, Artwork and Embroidery orders in the sales person's name — choose the sales person under Who brought this order? — or for a freelance sales person.
  • Credit a new client to a sales person only when that person confirms they brought the client; a client belongs to one person only.
  • Price strictly from the price list: services and materials as separate lines, artwork size, heat-press fee, stitch counts, and the supplier's quote on contracted-out work. Customer name and phone are recorded for repeat and credited clients.

Taking payment

  • Take payment by cash, M-Pesa (STK push), bank transfer or card, singly or split, and record each one on the order with its reference.
  • Record deposits and balances as they arrive. Every shilling received at the counter is entered in the system the same day; nothing is held or accepted outside it.
  • If an M-Pesa confirmation does not arrive, confirm by hand only with the 10-character code from the customer's SMS; a code already used on another payment is refused.
  • Print the receipt on the 80 mm thermal printer (two copies) and send receipts and invoices by email or WhatsApp where asked.
  • Work the Payments list of pending balances and remind customers and sales persons of what is owing.

End of day and reconciliation

  • At close, balance what you received (cash, M-Pesa, bank, card) against the payments recorded in the system, and report any difference at once.
  • Upload the M-Pesa statement and match payments in Accounting → M-Pesa Matching; refer exceptions you cannot match to Finance.

Handing over and keeping customers informed

  • Tell customers where their order is (Order Received → In Production → Quality Check → Ready → Completed) and when to collect.
  • Hand over only orders that have passed quality control (stage Ready for Pickup/Delivery). If a balance is owing, collect it first; hand over on credit only as management allows.
  • Pass corporate-client requirements to Finance, who prepare quotations and invoices.

3Authority and limits

What you can do in the system is set by your role and is checked on the server at every request.

You can

  • Capture orders in a sales person's name, and for freelancers
  • See every order
  • Take and record payments by any method
  • Confirm an M-Pesa payment by hand, with the SMS code
  • Print, email or WhatsApp receipts and invoices
  • Hand over finished orders
  • Record DTF film sales and artwork jobs
  • Match M-Pesa payments in Accounting

You need approval for

  • Discounts above the standard ceiling (supervisor)
  • Artwork or embroidery priced below the recommended price (manager)
  • Refunds, credit notes and corrections to a recorded sale (Finance)
  • Handing over an order on credit (as management allows)
  • M-Pesa items you cannot match or that are disputed (Finance)

You cannot

  • See supplier costs, mark-ups or other people's commission
  • Open Finance, Compliance or the rest of Accounting
  • Change prices, bands, settings or staff
  • Edit or delete a recorded payment
  • Approve your own approvals

4Performance measures

5Remuneration

  • A fixed monthly salary as set out in your contract of employment, run through payroll each month with PAYE, NSSF, SHIF and the housing levy deducted; your payslip shows each line.
  • Your salary is recorded on your staff record by Finance (Compliance → Employees).

6Standards and conduct

  • Your PIN is yours. Never share it, never sign in as someone else, and sign out when you leave the screen.
  • Every record is traceable. The system records who captured, changed or approved each order, payment, expense and claim; accuracy and honesty in what you enter are part of the job.
  • Money is recorded as it is received. No side payments, no holding customer money outside the system, no unrecorded discounts.
  • Credit the sale to the right person. Never move an order to another sales person, or to a freelancer, to give or deny credit.
  • Customer and staff information is confidential and used only for the work it was given for.
  • Raise problems early — a wrong figure, a disputed payment, a control that is not being followed — with your manager.

8Typical requirements

  • Excellent customer care, a calm manner and clear communication.
  • Numeracy and care with money: cash handling, change, M-Pesa and balancing at close.
  • Accuracy and honesty in recording; comfortable with a computer or tablet and a till-style screen — training is given.
  • Organised and able to handle several customers and calls at once.
  • Experience in reception, cashiering or retail is an advantage.
